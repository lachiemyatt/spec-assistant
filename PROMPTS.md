# Example prompts

Copy, tweak, send. Each one assumes your product register is uploaded to the project.

## Options

> Give me 3 interior finish palettes for levels 1 to 9 of a commercial office fit-out. Level 1 is reception and client meeting rooms, levels 2 to 9 are open-plan workspace with breakout areas. Palettes: warm, neutral, bold.

> Same brief, but give me budget tiers A / B / C instead, using the same palette direction for all three.

> Level 4 is a wellness floor. Suggest 2 palettes that feel softer than the rest of the building but still belong to palette B.

## Schedule

> Turn palette B into a finish schedule for these rooms: [paste room list with level, number and name].

> Add a CHECK: note against every wet area and every floor finish that needs a slip rating.

## Revit export

> Give me that schedule as a Revit export.

(The CSV opens straight in Excel. Keep it: it is the input for the Tier 2 Dynamo script on the roadmap.)

## Spec

> Write spec clauses for every finish in palette B, following the uploaded firm template.

## Admin

> Turn these notes into meeting minutes with an actions table (who / what / by when): [paste notes].

> Draft an RFI to the joinery contractor asking for a substitute for WF-03, which has a 12 week lead time.

## Starting from supplier sheets instead of a register

> I've uploaded 6 supplier PDFs. Build me a product register in the same format as product-register.csv, assigning codes FL-, WF-, CF-, SK- in order. Only include products actually in the PDFs.
